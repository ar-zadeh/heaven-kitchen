# Data

## Participant data (not included)

The analyzed dataset (23 participants, two sessions each) and the raw gaze and screen recordings are not released, to protect participant privacy. De-identified data are available from the corresponding author on request, subject to IRB approval (Penn State IRB STUDY00022856).

## Synthetic data

`synthetic/Participants_SYNTHETIC.csv` has the same columns and design as the analyzed dataset: 23 participants × 2 sessions, 11 in the AI-feedback condition (6 male, 5 female) and 12 in the control condition (6 male, 6 female). Its scores are random draws around the group means and standard deviations reported in the paper; it contains no participant's data. It is generated by `analysis/make_synthetic_data.R`.

Use it to check that the analysis code runs. Results computed from it do not reproduce the paper.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Participant ID` | Participant number |
| `Session` | 1 = training session (LPFG feedback or control); 2 = post-training session (no feedback for anyone) |
| `Gender` | 1 = male, −1 = female |
| `AI-Feedback` | 1 = received LPFG feedback in Session 1; −1 = control |
| `Total Served` | Customer groups served (primary outcome) |
| `Total Failed` | Customer groups not served within 120 s |
| `Money Left` | Money remaining at the end of the session ($) |
| `Net Money` | 63 × `Total Served`, as computed in the original spreadsheet |
| `Time` | Session length in seconds; 300 is the full 5-minute session, and shorter values mean the participant ran out of money or ingredients |
